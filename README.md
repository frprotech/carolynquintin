# Carolyn Quintin, homepage concept

A homepage concept for [carolynquintin.com](https://carolynquintin.com) that gives **LinkedIn for Business** an equal place next to Career Coaching and Leadership Training. It is a reference for her web designer to rebuild in Showit.

Open `index.html` in a browser. Previews: `screenshots/desktop.jpg`, `screenshots/mobile.jpg`.

## Structure

```
index.html
assets/css/styles.css
assets/js/main.js        sticky header, mobile menu, testimonial slider, reveal on scroll, form states
assets/img/              logo, badge, favicons, portraits, service photos, client logos (tinted navy), testimonial photos
```

## Page

1. Header with centred logo
2. Hero with Carolyn's portrait
3. Client logos
4. Three services, equal cards
5. Career band: "Feeling stuck in a role that no longer excites you?" (from the current homepage)
6. LinkedIn for Business: what owners get, the free 10-minute profile review, a client quote
7. Meet Carolyn
8. Testimonials slider, ten clients across all three services
9. Contact band
10. Footer modelled on the current one: Navigate, CQ badge, Follow and newsletter signup

## Brand

Turquoise `#2CA5A3`, navy `#233D4D`, blush `#FDE8E2`, off-white `#FAFAFA`, white. Lustria for headings, Libre Franklin for text. Square corners, 1px outlines, real photography.

## Notes for the Showit build

- Forms are layout only. Connect the contact form and the newsletter form to her Kit / Showit forms.
- Favicons are generated from her current site icon (the CQ badge).
- Client logos were her white PNGs, recoloured navy.
